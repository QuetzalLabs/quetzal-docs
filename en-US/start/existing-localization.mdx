## Integrating With Quetzal

If your codebase has an existing localization system, Quetzal can maintain it moving forward.

Quetzal will adhere to your existing practices to update and audit localization.

You can begin by [linking your codebase](/en-US/install/linking-a-repository). This will create an integration to provide localization uppdates to your code via git.

## What Will Quetzal Do?

Once linked, Quetzal will translate future content, verify the quality of existing translations, and update your code to ensure full translation coverage.

This integration currently only supports static content. Content generated at runtime, translated or otherwise, will not be affected by this integration. To translate runtime content, you can use your API.

Features can be toggled in settings once a repository is linked.

## Compatibility

To make sure your stack works with Quetzal ahead of time, you can [check compatibility with Quetzal](/en-US/install/is-my-stack-supported).
